📘 How To Use BITCAGS Academy

Your step-by-step guide to getting started

1

Register

  • Click "Register" top right.
  • Fill in your details (Name, Gmail, DOB, Password, Photo URL).
  • Select your course and experience level.
  • Review and click "Confirm & Register".
  • Save your unique App ID.
  • Or type register me in AI chat.
2

Login

  • Go to "Student Login".
  • Enter your App ID and password.
  • Click "Login to Academy".
  • You'll see your dashboard with courses, certs, and admission.
3

Enroll & Pay

  • Browse courses on the home page.
  • Click "View Curriculum" then "Begin Study Pathway".
  • Choose Pay with Paystack or Local Payment.
  • After payment, the course appears in your dashboard.
4

Lessons & Quizzes

  • Go to dashboard and click "Enter Lecture Room".
  • Select a lesson from the sidebar.
  • Watch the video and read the content.
  • Click "Generate 30-Question Exam" to take the quiz.
  • Score 60%+ to unlock the next lesson.
5

Certificates & Admission

  • Complete all lessons to see the "Get Certificate" button.
  • View your professional certificate with CEO and Tutor signatures.
  • From dashboard, go to "Admission" tab.
  • Select your course and click "Generate Admission Letter".
6

AI Assistant

  • Click "AI Bot" in the navigation.
  • Ask about courses, lessons, or registration.
  • Type register me for conversational registration.
  • Use "Generate AI Explanation" inside a lesson.
7

Verify Credentials

  • Go to the "Verify" page.
  • Enter the Credential ID (e.g., BTC-CR-XXXXX) or Admission Number.
  • Click "Search" to verify authenticity.
  • The system displays the student's name, course, and status.
